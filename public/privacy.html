Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

The short version: Rolligan does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

Information we collect

None. Rolligan does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, or any other personal information. We do not use analytics or advertising software development kits, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Multiplayer games

When you host or join a multiplayer game, the display name you type and the live game state (scores, the current pot, whose turn it is) are sent in real time to the other players in your game through a temporary connection identified by your room code. This information is broadcast only for the duration of the game and is not written to any database, retained, or used for any other purpose. When the game ends, it is gone.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Multiplayer Host" unlock is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; Rolligan never sees or stores your payment details. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Rolligan is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the date above.
